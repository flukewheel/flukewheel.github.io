# Reaction cursor during saves

Submitted: 31 October 2026

The project owner reported that the crossed-circle cursor still appeared at the instant of clicking Bookmark, Love and Dislike, even though the actions saved. The earlier cursor-style override did not eliminate the native disabled-button transition.

## Changed

- Keep the three reaction buttons natively enabled during an in-flight mutation. Mark them `aria-disabled` and the control group busy while a synchronous lock ignores duplicate clicks.
- Keep the dislike-reason trigger natively enabled during the same interval, but ignore attempts to open it until the mutation finishes.
- Prevent icon SVGs from intercepting pointer events or being selected during a click.

## Verified

- Desktop and mobile browser checks held each mutation in flight and confirmed the Bookmark, Love and Dislike buttons stay natively enabled with a pointer cursor, then save successfully. A second click while Bookmark is busy produced no duplicate write.
- Lint, typecheck, six unit tests and the production build passed.
