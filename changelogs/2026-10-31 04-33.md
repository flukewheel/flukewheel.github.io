# Working bookmark collection

Submitted: 31 October 2026

The project owner reported that the bookmark icon showed a disabled cursor and saved sites were absent from the account menu's Bookmarks section. Bookmark actions are available when an approved discovery has loaded in the viewer; the icon remains disabled on home and settings. The Bookmarks route had still been a placeholder.

## Changed

- Replaced the signed-in Bookmarks placeholder with an owner-scoped collection, paginated 20 at a time.
- Display saved websites with links back to their viewer routes and controls to remove them. Unavailable catalogue entries remain removable.
- Refresh the collection after a bookmark is saved or removed, including actions resumed after authentication.
- Added a browser journey that saves a bookmark, finds it in the collection, removes it, and checks the viewer state.

## Verification

Lint, typecheck, six unit tests, the production build and all 30 desktop/mobile browser checks passed, including save, list, remove and viewer state. The hosted account still needs a live owner check after deployment.
