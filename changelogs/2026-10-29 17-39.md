# Inline-only discovery viewer

Submitted: 29 October 2026 at 17:39 Europe/London

- Removed the external-opening fallback. Every available catalogue entry now routes to Flukewheel's inline iframe viewer, regardless of its stored embedding status.
- Allowed framed sites to retain their own origin while keeping the iframe restrictions on popups and top-level navigation. This lets several script-driven sites run in the viewer.
- Changed the copy action to share the Flukewheel discovery URL instead of the source site's URL.
- Marked all six active local seed entries `allowed` and applied the same values to the running local database. The two inactive entries remain under URL/certificate review.
- Verified all six active viewer routes render an iframe. Third-party limitations remain: Radio Garden displays its own “Embedding Not Allowed” message, and the HTTP-only Your Scene Sucks URL will be blocked as mixed content when Flukewheel is hosted over HTTPS.
