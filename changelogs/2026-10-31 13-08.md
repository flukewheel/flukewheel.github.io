# Phase 4 local submission and moderation checkpoint

Submitted: 31 October 2026

Started Phase 4 with a guest suggestion form, server-side intake, a private admin review queue, transactional approval and rejection, and catalogue/category management. Submitted pages are held for manual review; the server does not fetch untrusted URLs without enforceable destination controls. Viewer behavior remains inline only.

The work is a local checkpoint. Hosted deployment and live administrator verification remain before these features are available on flukewheel.github.io. See `docs/phase-4-progress.md` for the implemented scope, verification, and remaining work.
