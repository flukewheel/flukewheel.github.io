# Saved action feedback in the viewer

Submitted: 31 October 2026

The project owner reported that a bookmark could appear not to save and asked for visible progress and saved states on the viewer actions.

## Changed

- Bookmark, Love and Dislike show an animated spinner while their write is in progress. Bookmark becomes a green tick, Love becomes a filled red heart, and Dislike becomes filled in its outline colour after a successful write. Selecting a dislike reason keeps the filled state.
- Keep the saved state visible for the current discovery and show each newly selected discovery's own state after Bump. Repeated clicks on an already saved action no longer remove it inadvertently.
- Update the interaction cache immediately after a successful write and show save errors next to the controls.

## Verified

- The full 30-test browser suite passed on desktop and mobile, including delayed writes, duplicate clicks, dislike reasons, sign-in handoff, and Bump.
- Lint, typecheck, 12 unit tests, and the production build passed.
